Init — About

I’m a Computer Science graduate from Maharishi Dayanand University, based in Delhi. My favourite part of tech is where code meets the real world: sensors, wires, blinking lights, and the occasional board that refuses to work until it’s wired just right. I enjoy the slow, satisfying part too, tuning something until it runs smoothly in real time.

I like owning a project from the first wire to the final screen, and I learn fastest when something breaks and I have to find out why. When I’m not wiring things up, I’m solving DSA problems in C++ on LeetCode. Next on my list: teaching tiny devices to think (TinyML).
